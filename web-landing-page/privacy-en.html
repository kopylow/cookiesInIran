3. Comments

When you post a comment we process:

  • Name (required, free choice, max 60 chars).
  • Comment text (required, max 2000 chars).
  • Email (optional). Used only for (a) the 6-digit verification code used to claim your name and (b) optional reply notifications.
  • Hashed IP address (SHA-256 with rotating salt). The plaintext IP is never stored. Purpose: anti-spam, rate limits.
  • Session cookie (cii_sid, HttpOnly, 1 year). Set only after successful email verification so you can keep posting under the same name without re-entering the code. Strictly necessary for a feature you actively requested (no banner required).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent by submitting the comment) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in spam protection).

4. Cloudflare Turnstile

To prevent automated abuse we use Cloudflare Turnstile. The script is loaded only when you click into the comment field. Turnstile evaluates browser signals to detect humans without setting tracking cookies.

5. Email delivery

Verification codes and reply notifications are sent via Resend (Resend.com, EU region). Email addresses are hashed (SHA-256 with a stable salt) and additionally encrypted with AES-GCM at rest.

6. Retention

  • Pending verifications: 24 hours.
  • Resolved reports: 90 days.
  • Comments: until you request deletion.
  • Bans (bans table): indefinite, for abuse prevention.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). On a deletion request we replace the body and name of your comment with "[deleted]" while keeping the thread structure intact. You may lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.
